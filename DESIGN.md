# Design System: hack::peel

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Assembly Board, powered"**

The site is one circuit board with the power on. The page ground is matte soldermask, and under the mask you can see the board's copper wherever the board is empty: buses of traces that turn in 45-degree steps and drop into vias, drawn a few steps lighter than the board, laid around the content and never under a word. Type and outlines are white silkscreen. Copper cyan appears only where the board carries signal: the spine trace and its vias, LEDs, links, focus, a pulse running along a buried trace, the logo's colons, and the one copper pour that closes the home page.

The brand is the original. The logo is the one the live site draws: "hack::peel" in Space Grotesk bold, white, with the colons in copper cyan; in the home hero the colons glow and a copper bar sits centred under the name, exactly as in `public/full-logo.png`. Space Grotesk, the logo's face, now sets every word on the site, and JetBrains Mono, the live site's second face, sets times, years, prices and step numbers.

The countdown is still the one signature part: a clock chip whose face is a live LED dot matrix. On wide screens its pins are wired into the board: the right-hand pins fan out and run off to the board's edge, two of them carrying signal in, and the left-hand pins drop straight into vias. Each inner page's head has its own field of routing coming in from the right edge; the empty space under each section heading has a patch of routing; trunks run down both page margins, more of them on wider screens. Buttons are square switch bodies on a darker plinth that press down. The organiser and the "Why", values and conduct reasons sit in silkscreen footprints, and the sponsors ride a reel of carrier tape, the tape that feeds parts to a pick-and-place machine. FAQ rows end in DIP switches, the registration steps are an LED bar graph, the About history is a routed trace with vias, and the footer is an edge connector.

Rejections that stand: the earlier "timing sheet" look with its ambient glow, dot field and shimmer button; and the board decoration the client found weird in the first build: part designators, the nav's pad row, side pads on buttons, mounting holes, hero traces running to test points, a tall decorative chip, IC packages around plain contact details. The copper added in R8 and R9 is different in kind: tone-on-tone routing in the empty board behind the content, never labelled, never crossing text. The client asked for more of it, not less: the background should not read as empty.

Motion is a power-up, then a live board. On load the spine draws down, the hero reads in, the logo wipes on and its colons blink on and then breathe, and the clock scans once and ticks dot by dot. While they are on screen, a few buried traces carry a short cyan pulse, and the sponsor tape feeds slowly. All of it sits behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and the `ATMOSPHERE_ON` switch in `src/data/site.ts` turns it all off by putting `html.calm` on the root. With motion off the board is drawn once and holds still, and the tape becomes a still strip you can scroll.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One eleven-step neutral ramp at hue 238. Every grey is one of its steps.
- Copper cyan is the only accent, and it only ever means signal: trace, via, LED, link, focus, pulse, pour, and the logo's colons.
- A soldermask ground with copper routed under it through all the empty board (hero, page heads, under section headings, the margins), tone on tone; no noise, no grid, no glow blob.
- The original hack::peel logo, and its face, Space Grotesk, for every word. JetBrains Mono only for times, years, prices and step numbers.
- One signature part (the LED clock chip), wired into the board; the rest is a few working parts, footprints and plain type.
- A power-up on a single expo ease, then a quiet live board (logo breath, pulses, the tape), all switchable off.

## Colors

The palette is a board: soldermask black, copper under the mask, white silkscreen, copper cyan signal and pale pad metal, with nothing else.

### Primary
- **Copper Cyan** (signal): the brand cyan. It draws the spine trace and its vias, the routed timeline, the 404's live trace, the nav's current-page underline, lit LEDs (sponsor tier dots, LED bar, DIP lamp and knob, clock matrix), the logo's colons and the bar under the hero logo, the part icons, the pulses on buried traces, the day tabs' cap edge, link text, focus rings, text selection, a sponsor pocket's hover edge and the footer's edge-connector fingers. It fills a surface only for the primary switch and the copper pour.
- **Hot Copper** (signal-bright): the hover state of cyan surfaces and links only.
- **White-hot Copper** (signal-hot): the instant a clock LED lights, read by the canvas and blended toward cyan as the dot settles. Never a fill or a text colour.
- **Pour Ink** (on-signal): text on cyan, the hatch lines inside the pour, and the body of the dark switch on the pour. **Pour Ink Hover** (on-signal-hover) is that switch's hover.

### Secondary
- **Pad Silver** (pad): bare metal. The clock chip's pins, the empty socket's pins on the coming-soon pages, and the hour pins of the header on the link-preview card. Nothing else is silver.
- **Buried Copper** (trace = n4) and **Buried Via** (trace-via = n5): routing seen through the soldermask. 2px traces in n4, 11px vias with an n5 ring on the ground colour. Dormant copper is never cyan; only a pulse running along it is.

### Tertiary
- **Fault Red** (danger): form errors and a failed copy only.

### Neutral
The ramp runs n0 (the dark of a part's body) to n10 (white silkscreen). The role aliases point into it:
- **Soldermask** (ground = n1): the page and header ground, and what shows through the tape's sprocket holes.
- **Raised Mask** (raised = n2): the shadcn card, muted and accent surfaces, the day-tab hover, and the carrier tape.
- **Part Body** (n0): the clock chip, field wells, the DIP housing, the day-tab body, row hover, sponsor-cell and organiser hover, the tape's pockets, the footer, popovers and the mobile sheet.
- **Cap** (n3): the raised cap on the selected day tab, and the tape's edges.
- **Board Rule** (line = n4) and **Strong Rule** (line-strong = n6): section rules, the stats strip's hairlines, table rules, pocket outlines (n4), the header and footer edges, unlit bar segments, unlit LED rings and pending timeline vias, and the empty socket's dashed outline.
- **Field Edge** (n7): input borders, the DIP housing and the chip's pin-1 ring.
- **Silkscreen** (ink = n10), **Silkscreen Dim** (ink-2 = n9), **Silkscreen Faint** (ink-3 = n8): text in three strengths.
- **Umbra** (umbra): only inside shadows.
- **Plinths** (plinth-signal, plinth-dark; n4/n5 for neutral switches): a switch's thick lower edge.

### Named Rules
**The One Ramp Rule.** Every neutral is one of n0 to n10 at hue 238. Nothing else is grey.

**The Copper Means Signal Rule.** Cyan is drawn only where a board would carry current or light, plus the logo's colons. Buried copper at rest is neutral (n4); cyan travels along it only as a pulse. Cyan is never a decorative tint, background wash or panel.

**The Lit LED Rule.** Light is emitted only by a part that is on: a tier dot, a lit bar segment, the open DIP lamp, the clock matrix dots, a pulse's soft halo, and the logo's colons (the live site's own glow, 10px breathing to 20px). A glow never spreads far past what emits it.

**Character:** Space Grotesk is the logo's own face and the live site's type: a geometric grotesk with engineered quirks that suits a student hackathon. Heads are bold with tight tracking; body text is regular, set in ink-2.

**The Brand Face Rule.** Every word is Space Grotesk, the logo's face. Weight carries hierarchy (400 body, 500 to 600 labels and item heads, 700 heads and figures); heads are sentence case with tight negative tracking. No uppercase labels, no eyebrows.

**The Mono Is Tabular Rule.** JetBrains Mono appears only on times, years, prices and step numbers.

## Layout

Content sits in one centred column, min(100% - 3rem, 76rem), widening its gutter to 2rem per side from 640px. The spine trace runs the full page height 1.1rem outside the column's left edge. Every section is a two-column split from 900px (4fr : 8fr, gap 1.25rem by 3rem) with padding clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem); each opens on a board rule with a via where it meets the spine.

Page heads are a full-width band that clips a field of buried routing pinned to its top right, as tall as the band, 880px wide and fading out (mask) toward the title; on phones the field sits at 45% opacity behind the title. The rest of the page's routing is laid by `BoardLayer.tsx` under everything in `<main>`: patches in each section's left column above and below its heading (from 900px), patches beside content that stops well short of the right column (the contact email and venue; never beside lists or tables), and trunks in both margins wherever they are 44px or wider (the left from the top of the page, the right from below the hero or page head), all ending above the copper pour. Section heads in a left column do not stick on scroll, so their patches stay clear. The home hero is a 12-column grid from 1024px: the copy (logo lockup, tagline, sub-copy, the two switches, date and venue) takes 7 columns, the clock chip the other 5, vertically centred, with its fan-out clipped at the page's right edge (`overflow-x: clip` on the hero). Below the hero: the stats strip (four equal figures in a ruled band), then "Organised By:" with the organiser panel, "Our Sponsors" with the tape reel, "Why hack::peel?" with its four reasons in a 2x2 list, and the copper pour.

## Elevation & Depth

The board is flat; copper under the mask is drawn on it, not raised above it. Depth means a part standing on the board: a short umbra cast downward, and a switch's plinth.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift**, **Pressed**, **Chip**, **Pour switch** and **Cap face** as before (see `src/index.css`).
- **Emission:** a lit dot (8px), the DIP lamp (7px), a lit bar segment (10px), the clock matrix (canvas blur), a pulse's halo (a 7px stroke at 30% under the 2px core), and the logo's colons (text-shadow 10px at 50%, breathing to 20px).

### Named Rules
**The Standing Part Rule.** A shadow means height above the board, cast down with a negative spread. Flat things (sections, rules, footprints, copper, the tape, text) never cast one.

**The Copper Under The Mask Rule.** The ground itself is a flat colour. The only marks on it are buried copper (the chip's fan-out, each page head's field, the patches and margin trunks), the hatch inside the copper pour, and the footer's edge fingers. Buried copper is tone on tone and unlabelled, fills the empty board rather than sitting in a corner of it, and keeps at least 34px clear of any words or parts (the page-head fields fade out instead).

## Shapes

PCB geometry: rectangles with tiny radii, circles for anything drilled or lit, square-cut bodies, and 45-degree routing.
- **Square** (0): switch bodies and footprints.
- **Pad** (1px): chip pins and LED-bar segments.
- **Field** (2px): inputs, the DIP knob, the day tabs' cap, the focus ring's corner, the step tag's clip.
- **Part** (3px): the clock chip, the DIP housing, the day-tab body, the empty socket, the tape's pockets.
- **Round** (50%): vias, LED dots, sprocket holes, the chip's pin-1 ring.

Silkscreen footprints are a 1px outline (ink at 24%) with two 11px, 2px corner marks. Traces are 2px with round caps and joins, 10px apart in a bus, turning only in 45-degree steps; a bus's ends are staggered in two rows (or stepped) so vias never touch.

**The Three Radii Rule.** Exactly 1px, 2px and 3px, plus 50% for circles and 0 for switch bodies. Nothing is pill-shaped.

## Components

### Logo
The live site's logo, as text: "hack" and "peel" in ink, "::" in signal, Space Grotesk 700 at -0.025em. The hero lockup adds the glow on the colons and a copper bar (1.34em by 0.042em, a horizontal fade transparent-cyan-transparent) centred 0.17em under the name, as in `public/full-logo.png`; the bar is the site's only gradient. The nav and footer logos are plain and turn cyan on hover.

### Buttons (plinth switches)
Unchanged: a square body (0), 3rem tall, padding 0 1.5rem, on a 3px plinth. Primary cyan, secondary outlined, dark on the pour, small in the header. Press: 2px down, plinth to 1px, in 0.04s.

### Navigation
A sticky n1 header, 4rem tall, n6 lower edge. Links 0.9375rem/500 in ink-2; the current page carries a 2px copper underline on the header's lower edge. Below 900px the links move into a right-side sheet.

### Copper routing (buried copper)
`src/components/board/Copper.tsx`. A bus is a centre line of straight runs in 45-degree headings; each trace is that line offset sideways (mitred at the bends) so the bus keeps its 10px pitch through a turn. Buses end in staggered vias, stepped vias, or run off the board. Page heads lay buses in horizontal lanes so none cross, seeded by the page's path so each page keeps its own routing. The clock chip's fan-out (`ChipFanout.tsx`) measures the chip's pins and routes from them. `BoardLayer.tsx` measures the page as drawn (the content box of each left-column heading, the inked extent of short right-column content, the margins either side of `.wrap`) and lays a patch (`columnBuses`: lanes of buses that start in vias at one side and end in vias before the other) or a set of margin trunks (`marginBuses`: up to four vertical buses in their own lanes, stepping sideways at 45 degrees) into each empty region, laying again when the page's size or content changes. Up to three traces per field carry a pulse (one per patch at most, one per trunk): a 26px cyan dash with a soft halo that runs the trace at 220px/s and then waits, only while the field is on screen.

### Sponsor reel (carrier tape)
A band of n2 tape with n3 edges and a row of sprocket holes (3.5px, ground colour, every 16px) along its top. Each sponsor sits in a pocket (n0, n4 outline, 3px radius, 5.5rem tall) on a 12rem cell, so the holes run on unbroken. It is Watermelon UI's Marquee (70s per pass, three copies, the copies after the first `aria-hidden` and `inert`), fading out at both ends, pausing under the pointer or keyboard focus, with a Pause/Play control under it. With motion off it is Watermelon UI's Scroll Fade: a still, keyboard-scrollable strip. A pocket's hover edge is cyan.

### Footprints and part icons
A footprint frames the organiser panel, the copy button, the footer's social icons, the menu button, every sponsor cell, and the part icons. A part icon is one of the live site's lucide icons (the "Why" reasons, the About values, the conduct principles) at 22px, 1.75 stroke, in cyan, inside a 2.75rem footprint. The organiser panel is a footprint linked as a whole: the team's mark at 7 to 9rem tall, its name at the Name step, and "Visit Our Website" as a text link; on hover it fills n0 and its marks and edge turn cyan.

One ease, expo out (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)). State changes 0.15s, switch press 0.04s. Power-up: the spine draws in 1.7s, the hero reads in over 0.9s, the logo wipes in over 1.1s, its colons blink on (1.2s) and then breathe (2.5s ease-in-out, 10px to 20px glow, as on the live site), and the chip's fan-out reads in after the chip. Live board: pulses run buried traces at 220px/s with a pause of about 4s between runs, only on screen; the sponsor tape feeds at one pass per 70s. Every animation is gated by `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and `html:not(.calm)`; under either, the tape becomes a still strip and nothing loops but the clock's values.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the original logo everywhere the name stands alone (nav, hero, footer, preview card), and set "hack::peel" as the logo inside heads.
- **Do** set every word in Space Grotesk, and keep JetBrains Mono for times, years, prices and step numbers.
- **Do** pick every neutral from the n0 to n10 ramp, by its role alias where one exists.
- **Do** reach for the existing parts first: a plinth switch, a DIP switch, an LED dot or bar, a footprint, a part icon, buried copper, the tape, the socket.
- **Do** fill the empty board with buried copper, tone on tone (n4 traces, n5 vias), 45-degree, unlabelled, keeping 34px clear of words and parts.
- **Do** give anything that moves on its own a way to stop: pause on hover and focus, a visible control, and a still version for reduced motion and `ATMOSPHERE_ON = false`.
- **Do** keep to the three radii, plus 50% for circles and 0 for switch bodies.
- **Do** start each section on a board rule with a via on the spine.

### Don't:
- **Don't** bring back part designators (J1, U1, SW1, TP1), pad rows under the nav, side pads on switches, mounting holes, test points, pinout leads, a decorative chip in a section's left column, or IC packages around plain content; the client found them weird.
- **Don't** run copper under or across text, beside full-width rules (lists, tables), over the copper pour, label it, or light it at rest.
- **Don't** bring back the timing-sheet atmosphere: no ambient glow blobs, radial washes, dot field, grid background, or shimmer sweep.
- **Don't** add a gradient other than the bar under the hero logo, a second accent hue, or a cyan-tinted panel.
- **Don't** glow anything that is not lit (an LED, a pulse, the logo's colons).
- **Don't** use pill shapes, radii above 3px, soft rounded cards, or a card kit with drop shadows.
- **Don't** use labels as kickers or eyebrows above headings.
- **Don't** add looping motion that cannot be stopped, or animate anything when `.calm` is set.
