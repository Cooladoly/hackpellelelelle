## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is one circuit board, and every control is a part you could solder. It refuses both the neon-glow dark template and the earlier hairline timing sheet: no ambient light blobs, no card kit, no generic dot grid; the board's own material does the work.

OWN-WORLD: Matte soldermask black from one eleven-step neutral ramp (hue 238, lightness 0.105 to 0.965); white silkscreen for every outline, label and designator; copper cyan #00bfff only for traces, pads, LEDs and the single closing copper pour; pale pad silver for exposed metal. Parts: the nav is pin header J1 with a lit LED on the current page; buttons are SMD switches with side pads; panels are IC bodies with a pin-1 notch; FAQ rows are DIP switches; the day tabs are a jumper on a header; the history is a routed trace with vias and a dashed airwire across the gap; sponsor tiers are a bill of materials; the footer is an edge connector. Archivo expanded for silkscreen headlines, JetBrains Mono for part labels, a 5x7 dot-matrix face for the clock. Mounting holes mark the board corners from 1200px wide (there is no room for them in narrower gutters); no fiducials are drawn.

STORY: Free, student-run, 34 hours, Dec 12-13, Mississauga, run by an FRC robotics team. The board powers on and the clock chip counts down; the organiser, the venue and the sponsor wall are the parts list that makes it credible; the Register switch is the one closed circuit to click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width soldermask board with pin header J1 across the top. Left: the silkscreen wordmark hack::peel at about 7rem with the :: as two cyan pads, the tagline, the sub-copy, then Register Now (SW1, a cyan switch) and View Schedule (SW2). Right: chip U1, an IC body with pins, showing the countdown as a live 5x7 LED matrix (days:hours over minutes:seconds) with the date and venue under it. Cyan traces leave U1's pins toward SW1 and the 34-pin header (J2, the 34-hour ruler) along the base of the hero; the four stat figures sit on test points TP1 to TP4 below.

MOTION GRAMMAR: One power-up on the home page: traces draw out from J1 to each part, the LEDs light in order, the LED matrix scans once and then shows the time. After that a single pulse crosses the main trace every few seconds (paused off screen); traces draw themselves as sections scroll in; pointing at a part lights its LED and its trace; switches press down. The clock ticks by individual dots turning off and on. Everything is visible without JavaScript, every animation sits behind prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, and the ATMOSPHERE_ON switch in src/data/site.ts still removes all of it.

AS BUILT (after R7), where the build differs from the blocks above; the thesis holds, the vocabulary is pared back to the parts that do a job. The nav is plain links; the current page is underlined in copper on the header's lower edge. Switches are square bodies on a darker plinth that travel down when pressed, with no side pads. The hero is the wordmark (6rem cap, scaling from 1024px so it fits its column), tagline, sub-copy, the two switches and the date and venue; the clock chip sits beside it, vertically centred, with no traces leaving it. The four stats are one strip of equal figures divided by hairlines. The "Why" reasons are a two-by-two list, each with a lit LED dot. Contact is plain type. The day tabs are a segmented control whose raised cap slides between the days. Sections reveal by drawing their rule and fading in; the spine draws once on load; the clock scans once and ticks dot by dot. The coming-soon pages show an empty footprint and the 404 an open circuit. The board's ground is flat; the only hatch is inside the copper pour.

AS BUILT (after R8), where it differs from the paragraph above. The logo is the live site's (Space Grotesk 700, cyan colons; in the hero the colons blink on and then breathe, and the copper bar sits centred under the name), and every word is Space Grotesk. Copper is routed under the mask, tone on tone (n3 traces, n4 vias, 45-degree buses, staggered vias): on wide screens the chip's right-hand pins fan out to the board's edge and its left-hand pins drop into vias; each inner page head clips a field of buses laid in lanes and seeded by its path, fading out before the title; a few traces carry a 26px cyan pulse at 220px/s while on screen. The home page runs hero, stats, Organised By: (a full section head and a clickable organiser panel with the team's mark at 7 to 9rem), Our Sponsors (the carrier-tape reel, pausable, still under reduced motion), Why hack::peel? (each reason leads with the live site's icon on a footprint), the pour. About values and conduct principles lead with their live-site icons too. Section heads are 2rem to 3rem.

AS BUILT (after R9): the buried copper covers the whole page's empty board, not just its top. From 900px each two-column section's left column carries a patch of routing above or below its heading where there are at least 84px free (Organised By: is top-aligned so it has one), content that stops well short of the right column gets a patch beside it (the contact email and venue, never lists or tables), and wherever a margin is 44px or wider it carries trunks (one at 1440px, three or four at 1920px): the left margin from the top of the page, the right from below the hero or page head, both ending above the copper pour. Traces are n4 and vias n5. The site is otherwise as after R8.

FORM: Candidate 6 of 7 grounded directions (a PCB assembly board), assigned by the seed (key defcb39b) and built unattended after the decision page went unanswered for twelve minutes and the user said to keep working. Raises: one cyan trace runs the page's full height (from the glaze shelf); empty board is designed keep-out space (from the ikebana alcove); loudness sets scale, so 34 and the date are cut huge (from the bolted book); every neutral comes from one eleven-step ramp (from the exposure record).
